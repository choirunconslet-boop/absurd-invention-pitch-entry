# Absurd Invention Pitch Entry

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch"**
(`u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`).

`main.py` writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — a pitch for a
houseplant that calls in sick for you whenever you forget to water it,
so at least one of you finally gets a day off.

The pitch (121 characters, one sentence):

> A houseplant that calls in sick for you every time you forget to water it, so at least one of you finally gets a day off.
